Featured research · MSc thesis, University of Ghana, 2026

Measuring galamsey's air footprint from orbit

Galamsey strips forests and turns rivers such as the Pra, Offin and Ankobra brown. That damage is easy to see. The air pollution is harder to see: dust from pits and bare ground, and diesel fumes from excavators, pumps and generators. Ghana's air monitors sit in cities such as Accra and Tema, far from the mining districts, so I measured it from space.

  • 2Satellites: Sentinel-2 for land, Sentinel-5P for air
  • 52Districts: 30 in Ashanti, 22 in Western
  • 72Months, January 2019 to December 2024
  • 3,744District-months in one modelling panel

Method

  1. 01

    Collect

    Cloud-masked Sentinel-2 composites at 10 m and Sentinel-5P air readings at about 3.5 × 5.5 km, averaged over each district every month in Google Earth Engine.

  2. 02

    Combine

    Greenness (NDVI), bare soil (BSI), water (MNDWI) and moisture (NDMI) beside UV aerosol, NO₂ and SO₂. Clouds hid 17% of land months, mostly June to September.

  3. 03

    Score

    Pollution Risk Score = 0.5 × aerosol + 0.3 × NO₂ + 0.2 × SO₂. Equal or PCA weights give almost the same district ranking (Spearman 0.992 and 0.991).

  4. 04

    Predict and explain

    Random Forest and XGBoost on 12 land and calendar features. Trained on 2019 to mid-2023, tuned on the next 9 months, tested once on April to December 2024, with SHAP for explanations.

  5. 05

    Test

    Correlation and dose-response, difference-in-differences for the July 2021 crackdown, and five checks of whether the pollution really comes from mining, plus a wind test.

Code, notebook and Streamlit dashboard

Full title: AI-Based Spatiotemporal Monitoring of Illegal Artisanal Mining (Galamsey) and Associated Atmospheric Pollution in Ghana: A Multimodal Remote Sensing and Machine Learning Approach. Supervisor: Prof. Solomon Mensah, Department of Computer Science, University of Ghana.
